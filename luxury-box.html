Common questions

Macadamia nuts are imported — sourced via wholesale importers in Mumbai who receive them from Australia and South Africa. As an imported nut, they have a noticeably higher price point than domestic nuts, which is part of what makes the Luxury Box feel genuinely different from standard hampers.

The honey variety depends on seasonal availability at the time of sourcing. Options include Himalayan multiflora, Nilgiri wild forest, or Sundarbans mangrove honey — all raw, unfiltered, and origin-labelled. We'll confirm the specific variety included when we send your campaign quote.

Yes. A common setup is: Classic (₹1,499) or Diwali Hamper (₹2,999) for all employees + Luxury Box (₹9,999) for the leadership team. We handle mixed orders from one campaign. Mention the split in your form or WhatsApp message.

The box is MDF (medium-density fibreboard) with a wood-grain laminate finish — it looks and feels like wood without the fragility or weight variation of solid wood. This also makes it more consistent for bulk orders. The magnetic closure lid and foam tray are included. Company name is printed (or laser-engraved for orders above 50 units — confirm when ordering).

The Luxury Box has the longest lead time of all tiers — macadamia and honey sourcing require more advance notice. Submit your quote early and we'll confirm delivery timelines before you pay anything.
