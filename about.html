About Gift-it

We're building India's gifting infrastructure

Founded in Bengaluru, Gift-it is on a mission to make meaningful gifting effortless for every company in India — and every Indian company with a global team.

Why we built Gift-it

It started with a Diwali spreadsheet. Our team had spent years running people operations at fast-growing startups. Every year, the gifting season meant two weeks of coordinating hampers — matching names to addresses, chasing vendors, handling wrong deliveries, and reconciling expenses for finance. By the time the gifts arrived, the joy of the occasion had been replaced by exhaustion.

The problem wasn't unique to his company. Across India's fastest-growing startups, HR teams were burning days on what should have been a delightful experience. The global corporate gifting tools that existed were built for Western markets — they didn't understand Indian festivals, didn't ship to Tier 2 cities, and didn't integrate with the HRMS systems Indian companies actually use.

Giftly was built to fix this. We started with Diwali — the moment that exposes every gap in a company's gifting process — and are building toward a platform that covers every gifting moment that matters to Indian businesses, from employee recognition to customer loyalty.

Values

What we believe

Four principles that shape every decision we make — from product to support to pricing.

01

Gifting should feel personalized and thoughtful

A gift is a signal. It says: I see you, I appreciate you, you matter to this team. Automation should make that easier to deliver — not strip the feeling out of it. Every Gift-it feature is designed around the moment the recipient receives a gift, not around the sender's convenience.

02

India deserves first-class tools

Global SaaS was not built with Indian businesses in mind. Diwali isn't a custom config — it's the most important gifting event of the year for 500 million workers. GreytHR, Keka, and Darwinbox aren't edge cases — they're how India manages its people. Gift-it is built India-first.

03

Simplicity over features

We could build a thousand features. We choose to build the right ones. Our benchmark is whether an HR team at a 50-person startup can run a festival campaign without ever talking to us. If it requires a support ticket, we haven't finished building it.

Built for India

Made in Bengaluru. Delivered across India.

Most corporate gifting platforms were built for the US and adapted for India. We started from India and built outward. That means faster delivery, better integrations, and features that reflect how Indian companies actually work.
